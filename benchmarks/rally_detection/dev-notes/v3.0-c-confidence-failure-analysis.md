# v3.0-C confidence failure analysis

This analysis uses fresh v3.0-B detector output, the committed 142-rally
manifest, and the established official-window one-to-one matcher. It contains
no source paths or player names. Confidence is metadata for human review; it
does not create, remove, split, merge, or move candidates.

## Failure reproduced

The B score mostly measured motion magnitude. Across 170 official-window
candidates (115 matched, 55 unmatched), its aggregate ROC-AUC was 0.496,
tie-aware average precision was 0.654, and Brier score was 0.235. Matched
candidates had a lower median score than unmatched candidates in two of the
three scenes and in aggregate.

| Benchmark | Matched IQR / median | Unmatched IQR / median | ROC-AUC | Average precision | Brier |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 01 | .668-.765 / .710 | .550-.865 / .675 | .532 | .586 | .266 |
| 02 | .675-.835 / .740 | .590-.840 / .750 | .538 | .720 | .218 |
| 03 | .668-.743 / .710 | .718-.835 / .750 | .308 | .684 | .219 |
| Aggregate | .670-.795 / .710 | .590-.855 / .740 | .496 | .654 | .235 |

The inversion is clearest at the top: the aggregate old-score `>=.85` bucket
contained 30 candidates but only 15 matches (50% precision), below the
`.70-.85` bucket's 78.1%. In benchmark 03 only one of four candidates in the
old top bucket matched. Thresholding the old score therefore could not provide
a trustworthy review order: its best tested aggregate F1 was .743 at `.40`,
only slightly above the no-threshold .737 result.

## Feature findings

The useful evidence is structural rather than raw intensity:

- Dense-validated rescues were matched in all three scenes (2/2, 1/1, 2/2).
- Applied structural splits were also reliable (4/6, 13/15, 3/3).
- Moderate bilateral evidence was useful, but near-perfect symmetry was a
  repeatable background/camera-motion warning. Candidates above `.60`
  side-balance matched only 4/10, 6/10, and 1/6 by scene.
- Existing short-structure penalties enriched unmatched candidates in every
  scene, but duration alone was not safe: validated short rescues are real
  rallies and must remain able to score highly.
- Motion mean/peak, active ratio, audio density, and raw side balance were
  scene-specific or misleading. Audio remains context only and is not part of
  the calibrated score.

No single raw scalar separated all three scenes. The retained design therefore
uses a small fixed evidence formula: sustained support and strong duration,
target-like bilateral structure, validated rescue/split bonuses, and explicit
cautions for global symmetric motion and existing structural weakness.

## Cross-scene validation

The final score is an ordinal evidence score, not a fitted probability. As a
robustness check, a small deterministic parameter grid was evaluated with
leave-one-benchmark-out selection: for each held scene, parameters were chosen
using only the other two scenes, maximizing the weaker training-scene ROC-AUC
first and pooled training ROC-AUC second. Untouched held-scene ROC-AUCs were
`.670`, `.686`, and `.717`, versus old-score `.532`, `.538`, and `.308`. Mean
held-scene ROC-AUC improved from `.459` to `.691`.

This check supports the evidence directions across scenes, but the values
should not be interpreted as probability calibration. The UI and JSON state
that the score is ordinal and keep the old score as `rawConfidence`.
